# The Billionaire's Secret Wife - Ep 12 (draft v1)
INT. LIVING ROOM - MORNING
Sunlight fills the apartment. MAYA, 28, sets two cups of coffee on the table and looks out the window.
MAYA: Good morning. I made coffee, just the way you like it.
DANIEL: Thanks. I have a meeting later today, so I can't stay long.
MAYA: That's okay. I only wanted to talk for a minute about the dinner next week.
DANIEL: Sure. We can talk about it tonight if you want.
MAYA: Your mother called yesterday. She wants us to come early and help with the decorations.
DANIEL: That sounds nice. I'll check my calendar.
Maya smiles and sits down. Daniel scrolls through his phone.
MAYA: Daniel, is something wrong? You have seemed distant lately.
DANIEL: No, I'm just tired. Work has been busy.
MAYA: You never told me who Clara is.
Daniel's face goes still. His phone buzzes on the table.
DANIEL: Where did you hear that name?
MAYA: It was on your screen last night. She wrote that she misses you.
DANIEL: Maya, you don't understand. It isn't what you think.
MAYA: Then tell me the truth. Are you cheating on me?
DANIEL: I can't talk about this now.
MAYA: Who is she, Daniel?
The door opens. A WOMAN in a red coat steps inside.
WOMAN: Hello, Daniel. Did you tell her about the baby?
